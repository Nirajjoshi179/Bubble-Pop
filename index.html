How to play

  • Drag to aim, release to shoot. Bubbles bounce off the walls.
  • Match 3 or more of the same color to pop them. Bubbles left hanging fall for bonus points.
  • Tap the NEXT bubble to swap it with the one in your cannon.
  • Missing too often adds a new row. Don't let bubbles cross the dashed line!
  • Pop bubbles back-to-back for combos. Every 5th combo is a Super Boost (+2 moves).
  • Clear every bubble before you run out of moves.
